Interviews / Lourain Alhalabi

“You take a risk as a vulnerable artist”

An interview with Lourain Alhalabi

Prepared by Douri’s Empowering Creative Minds team · Edited by Jang Kapgen · July 16, 2026

Lourain Alhalabi is a young artist whose art is deeply inspired by her lived experiences. As a Syrian refugee, Alhalabi processes and shares her life back in Syria, as well as the journey that followed.
