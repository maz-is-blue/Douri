Co-Creation Journey Luxembourg

Expression is not a luxury. It never was. A new way of seeing.

Twelve artists from Luxembourg, Germany, Poland and Ukraine came together in a single room. Different languages. Different journeys.

One shared intention: to find a way of expressing what words alone cannot carry.
