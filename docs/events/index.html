Featured event

Final Forum — Empowering Creative Minds

Two years. Four countries. Countless stories turned into art. From Luxembourg to Germany, Poland and Ukraine, artists and communities came together to transform experience into form, silence into something visible. Now we gather one last time to celebrate this journey.

Exhibition: Walid El-Masri · Farshad Afsharimehr · Nathalie Lesure · Ecran Arslan

Performances: Valeria Khipatch · Agnieszka Sikorska · Tarek Al-Nabhan · Margaryta

Conferences: Art, trauma & mental well-being · Arts policy & funding in Luxembourg

Date26 & 27 June 2026
LocationMaison du Savoir, University of Luxembourg, Belval, Esch-sur-Alzette
EntryFree entry — registration required
